# Unable to Sign In — Troubleshooting Knowledge Article

**Article ID:** KB-003 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Audience:** CareConnect demonstration portal users  
**Status:** Demonstration sample

## Overview
Use this article when you cannot sign in to the fictional CareConnect Demo Portal. Complete the checks in order. If the issue continues, contact the organisation through its approved support channel.

## Before you start
Keep your password and verification codes private. Do not send them to support by email, chat, or ticket. Do not include personal or health information unless the organisation's approved process specifically requires it.

## Symptoms
- The sign-in page does not load.
- The portal says the email or password is not recognised.
- The verification step does not complete.
- The page returns to sign-in after you submit the form.

## Step 1: Confirm the official page
1. Open the portal using the link supplied by your organisation.
2. Check the web address for spelling errors.
3. If the page looks unexpected, do not enter your credentials; contact support using a known official channel.

**Expected result:** The recognised sign-in page appears.

## Step 2: Check the email address
1. Confirm that you entered the email address associated with the account.
2. Check for accidental spaces at the beginning or end.
3. Do not send your full credentials to support.

**Expected result:** The email field contains the correct account address.

## Step 3: Check service and browser status
1. Review the organisation's approved service-status notice.
2. Confirm that your browser is supported using the organisation's guidance.
3. If permitted, open a fresh browser session and try again.
4. Avoid using a public or shared device for account access where possible.

**Expected result:** You can reach the sign-in form and submit it without a browser-related error.

## Step 4: Use the approved recovery route
If the portal offers password recovery, follow the on-screen instructions. Make sure the recovery page is part of the official portal. Support staff should never ask you to disclose your current password or one-time code.

## Step 5: Contact support
If you still cannot sign in, contact the organisation using its approved support channel. Provide:
- The approximate time the problem occurred and your time zone.
- The page or step where the problem happened.
- The exact error wording, after checking it contains no sensitive information.
- Browser and device type, if requested and permitted.
- The checks you already completed.

Do **not** provide your password, one-time code, or unnecessary health information.

## When support should escalate
Support should use its approved escalation route if multiple users are affected, a service outage is suspected, the issue persists after approved checks, or there is any indication of unauthorised access or information exposure. Security/privacy concerns must not be handled as routine password troubleshooting.

## Accessibility and maintenance
Use descriptive headings, readable language, keyboard-accessible controls, meaningful alternative text for relevant images, and status descriptions that do not rely on colour alone. Validate the actual portal instructions and accessibility against the applicable requirements before publication.
